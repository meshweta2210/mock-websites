Our Story

Founded in 2009, XNC began with a simple vision: to make advanced therapeutic manufacturing accessible to innovative companies of all sizes. What started as a small operation has grown into a global leader in contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO) services.

We've built our reputation on scientific excellence, operational reliability, and a deep commitment to our partners' success. Today, we support over 50 active partners across multiple therapeutic modalities and development stages.
